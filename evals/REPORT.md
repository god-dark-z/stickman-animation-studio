## Per-agent and per-model

**No per-agent / per-model rows are recorded.** Measuring how a specific model writes a story
requires that model's API, and this project's rules forbid paid APIs. What *is* measured here is
everything the engine can prove without a model: the batteries, the gates, the resolver, the
normalizer, the voice chooser, the glyph coverage and the typecheck.

To add your own numbers, run a video with an agent, then append to `evals/model-runs.json`:

```json
{ "runs": [{ "agent": "Claude Code", "model": "sonnet", "lang": "en", "suite": "battery",
              "pass": 61, "total": 61, "source": "local run 2026-11-20", "date": "2026-11-20" }]}
```

Then re-run `npx tsx scripts/eval.ts` and the table above fills in.

## How to read this

- A **gate stack** row is one story rendered through sync → geometry → commonsense (+ Bangla
  text and glyph gates). 3/3 means every gate passed for that story.
- A **battery** row is the engine's ability to draw a sentence the way a human pictures it.
  It is a property of the engine and the glossary, not of any model.
- Nothing in this report was inferred. Rows that could not run say so.
